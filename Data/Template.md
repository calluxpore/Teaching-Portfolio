### 1. Teaching Philosophy
*Reflective summary grounded in four core pedagogical convictions:*
- **Experiential Learning Through Making:** Design as an action-biased practice; iterative cycles of making, testing, failing, and revising. Microcontroller prototypes, physical electronics, and custom LoRA style checkpoints.
- **Radical Accessibility of Computation:** Universal Design for Learning (UDL) and anti-oppressive studio culture. Demystifying code through live narrated programming in p5.js and Processing, treating computation as a pliable design medium.
- **Questioning as Method:** Speculative design, design fiction, and ethical critique of artificial intelligence. Framing provocations that resist rapid closure to foster intellectual independence.
- **Global Positionality & Ethic of Care:** Culturally responsive pedagogy informed by teaching across Canada, India, Germany, and Singapore. 40/60 weighting between process documentation and final artefacts.

### 3. Teaching Methodologies & Instructional Materials
- **Studio Rhythm:** Frame (20–30 min) → Guided Making (60–90 min) → Reflective Critique.
- **Live-Coding Pedagogy:** Real-time projected programming with narrated cognitive modeling and open debugging.
- **Project-Based Assessment:** Authentic rubrics weighting process documentation alongside outcomes in a 40/60 ratio.
- **Technology Toolkit:** Arduino/ESP32, Grasshopper 3D, Rhino Python, p5.js, ComfyUI, LoRA fine-tuning, Stable Diffusion, Unity 3D, Miro, Pitch, and GitHub.
- **Sample Syllabi On Record:** *Design For and With AI* (GEU), *Design Studio 1 - Lighting Design* (GEU), *Future Labs and Social Enterprise* (Pearl), *Introduction to 3D Printing at Scale* (UID).
